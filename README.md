# Rolfe Legends 3: World of Weirdos 👽🦆🧲

A farm roguelike deckbuilder made **by Wyatt, Aaron & Uncle James** — the third
Rolfe Legends game, and the first one the boys designed themselves.

**Play it:** https://jmoranii.github.io/rolfe-legends-3/

## The game

Fight weirdos across four wild worlds. Win pets. Bring them home to the barn.
Take them back out to battle beside you. Get stronger. Go weirder.

- **Four worlds:** The Crop Kingdom 🌽 · Critter Meadow 🐾 · Bricktopia 🧱 · The Kinetic Sandbox 🧲
- **Three heroes:** Wyatt the Speedy ⚡ · Aaron the Strong 🌪️ · Liam the Little 🍼
- **18+ pets** to win from battles — every one fights differently, and yes, you can pat them all
- **Duck bosses.** Diver, Brownie, and Harmless are Wyatt's real ducks. They have gone rogue. Calm them.
- **The Magnet Menace** waits at the end. The boys designed the fight themselves: knock the sand off, and you get ONE turn while the Magnet lies helpless. It throws magnets that hit for FIFTY. They were very clear about the fifty.
- **The farm remembers.** Coins and pets stay yours, win or lose. The shop sells barn expansions and the Battle Buddies unlock.
- **The Weirdness ladder.** Beat the game and the worlds can always get weirder. How far can you climb?

## The design credits

- **Aaron:** the whole pet loop (fight → win pets → barn, capacity 5 → shop upgrades → pets battle beside you), the worlds ladder, the Kinetic Sand Monster's detachable limbs and returning magnets
- **Wyatt:** cards like RL2, the duck bosses (one per world), the bear who hands you his own card every turn, the sand-shed + helpless-magnet finale phase, "make it harder than RL2, I want it to take a long time"
- **Uncle James (with Hugo):** everything that made their ideas real

## Tech

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, zero dependencies, no build step. Pure engine + 1,800+ unit
tests, a selfplay balance harness that plays thousands of runs, Playwright e2e in
Chromium and WebKit. Offline-first PWA (add it to your home screen — it works
with no internet after the first visit). Art: GPT-Image, storybook gouache.
Music: Suno, with word-synced karaoke credits.
