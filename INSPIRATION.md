# INSPIRATION.md — the steal list

James's directive (Sun 2026-08-16): find mechanisms from popular indie games that slot into Wyatt and Aaron's ideas, and use them as **strong inspiration**. Mechanics are not copyrightable; names, text, and art are 100% ours (the RL2 rule). Each entry: what the game does → what we steal → which of the boys' ideas it serves.

## The core loop steals

1. **Hades — the persistent home base between runs.** You die, you return home, home grows: new decorations, new conversations, permanent upgrades bought with meta-currency. **Steal:** the FARM is our House of Hades. Every run ends back at the farm; pets you won stay in the barn; the shop spends meta-currency (💰 "Farm Coins"?) on permanent things. This is the single biggest structural steal — it's exactly what Aaron described (barn that persists, shop that upgrades it) without knowing the genre term is "meta-progression."

2. **Wildfrost — companions who fight beside you.** Your deck contains *creatures* that deploy onto the field with their own HP, attacks, and countdown timers; you and your companions win together. **Steal:** an equipped PET is a companion in the fight — visible on your side of the battlefield with its own little action. Serves Aaron's "buy the access to let your pets fight with you."

3. **Wildfrost/Monster Train — units that inject their own cards.** Monster Train's units come with card synergies; Inscryption's creatures ARE cards. **Steal (Wyatt's own invention, validated):** each pet adds its **signature cards** to your deck while equipped — Bear adds *Claw Scratch* (2 hits × 6). Collecting pets literally changes what your deck can do. This is the pets-matter mechanic.

4. **Pokémon / Cassette Beasts — win a battle, chance to catch.** Beat a wild creature → chance it joins you; a collection screen tracks what you've found vs. what exists. **Steal:** Aaron's "random chance to get different kinds of pets" after winning fights, plus a **Barn Book** collection screen (silhouettes for pets not yet won — kids love completing a dex). Rarity tiers give the chase longevity.

5. **Rogue Legacy / Vampire Survivors — the permanent-upgrade shop.** Between runs, spend banked gold on upgrades that never go away (more capacity, new abilities). **Steal:** Aaron's shop exactly — barn capacity 5→more, "pets can battle" unlock, maybe pet training. Buying feels like progress even after a lost run.

## The structure steals

6. **Slay the Spire (via RL2) — the whole combat chassis.** Energy, intents, draft-as-you-go, relics, the node map. **Steal: our own RL2 codebase.** The engine, selfplay harness, test rig, music/credits/farmcode/sw systems are proven and ours. RL3 = RL2's chassis + the pet/farm/worlds layer on top.

7. **Spelunky / Dead Cells — themed worlds as an unlock ladder.** Distinct biomes with their own enemy casts, unlocked in sequence, each raising difficulty. **Steal:** the boys' themed worlds ("the more worlds you get to, the more good things unlock") — each world has its own weirdo bestiary, its own look/music, its own duck boss, and unlocks the next.

8. **Octopath Traveler / FF13 — the break-stagger window.** Chip a foe's shield → it's STAGGERED and helpless for a beat → you unload your biggest combo. **Steal (Wyatt independently invented this):** the Kinetic Sand Monster — 50 damage knocks all the sand off, the magnet "lays there helpless" for ONE turn, you deal max damage to its 100 HP. Consider reusing stagger as a rare mechanic on one enemy per world so the finale's mechanic is foreshadowed.

9. **StS Reptomancer / Awakened One — parts that fight and phases that return.** Summons that must die separately; bosses that re-form. **Steal (Aaron's limb design):** knocked-off limbs become their own enemies; defeated limbs sink back into the body; cleared limbs expose the magnet core that throws 50-damage magnets. It's Reptomancer × Awakened One × break-stagger — genuinely novel as a combination.

## The delight steals

10. **Inscryption — creatures with personality.** Every creature card feels alive (sigils, quirks, voice). **Steal:** every pet has one personality quirk that shows in play (a line, an animation, a sound) — Rusty barks at treasure, the Bear naps between fights. Pets should feel like *pets*, not stat blocks.

11. **Hades — the collection is emotional, not just numeric.** Petting Cerberus does nothing and everyone does it. **Steal:** you can visit and PAT every pet in the barn. No mechanical effect. Non-negotiable.

12. **RL1/RL2 canon (our own games) — the traditions.** Zero-hint secret (2-for-2 with the boys), synced-lyric Suno victory anthems, Coach James tips, Goldie, family cameos, Farm Code saves. All return. The victory song is already a locked boys' requirement.

## Anti-steals (considered, rejected)

- **Balatro's score-exponent math** — too abstract for the fairness-reads-on-screen pillar.
- **Inscryption's meta-horror** — wrong register for this audience, obviously.
- **Monster Train's two-resource lane defense** — a second spatial system on top of pets + worlds is too much new at once; the StS chassis stays.
